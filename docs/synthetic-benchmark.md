# Synthetic Tekton Recovery Benchmark

Date: 2026-10-03

This benchmark measures real Tekton executions using controlled synthetic
tasks. It compares a full PipelineRun restart with a recovery PipelineRun
generated by the prototype. No result values are fabricated.

## Repeated measured matrix

- kind v0.27.0, Kubernetes v1.31.4, Tekton Pipelines v1.6.0
- Linear pipelines containing 5 or 10 tasks
- Early, middle, and late injected failures
- Ten paired trials per configuration (60 paired trials total)
- Identical 0.2-second synthetic work in each task
- Container image warmed before measurement

| Tasks | Failure | Mean full restart | Mean recovery | TaskRuns avoided | Mean time reduction (95% CI) |
|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 5 | Early | 24.083 s | 19.512 s | 1/5 (20%) | 18.79% [15.79, 21.79] |
| 5 | Middle | 22.177 s | 12.979 s | 2/5 (40%) | 41.28% [36.78, 45.78] |
| 5 | Late | 23.676 s | 4.829 s | 4/5 (80%) | 79.58% [78.92, 80.24] |
| 10 | Early | 49.919 s | 45.344 s | 1/10 (10%) | 8.93% [5.05, 12.81] |
| 10 | Middle | 50.496 s | 30.417 s | 4/10 (40%) | 39.75% [38.25, 41.26] |
| 10 | Late | 52.004 s | 5.125 s | 9/10 (90%) | 90.15% [89.81, 90.48] |

The controlled results show the expected relationship: later failures permit
more safe inheritance, reducing both executed TaskRuns and observed recovery
time. These measurements support a limited synthetic-workload claim only.

The repeated benchmark completed successfully in [GitHub Actions run
37165598217](https://github.com/pratik0789/declarative-retry-tekton/actions/runs/37165598217).
Its 60 raw paired trials and grouped summary are published under
`results/synthetic-ci-60-trials-kind-v1.31.4-tekton-v1.6.0/`. The original
18-trial local dataset remains available under
`results/synthetic-linear-kind-v1.31.4-tekton-v1.6.0/`.

Confidence intervals use the two-sided Student t interval for ten paired-trial
percentage reductions (`df=9`, `t=2.262`).

## Limitations

- Ten repetitions per configuration support a controlled synthetic-workload
  comparison but not a production-performance claim.
- Pipelines are linear and tasks are intentionally small.
- Kubernetes scheduling overhead is a large portion of the measured duration.
- The benchmark does not represent eBay production workloads.

## Suggested paper wording

> Across 60 paired executions on kind and Tekton Pipelines v1.6.0, declarative
> recovery reduced executed TaskRuns in direct proportion to the safely
> reusable prefix. For late failures, recovery avoided 80% of TaskRuns in the
> five-task pipeline and 90% in the ten-task pipeline; mean observed recovery
> time was 79.58% and 90.15% lower, respectively. Early failures offered less
> reuse and correspondingly smaller reductions. These results characterize
> controlled synthetic workloads and should not be interpreted as production
> savings.
